# Test Case: m086-POS-01

### Test Case ID: m086-POS-01
- **Human Thinking Model**: Organizational Feedback Loop Analysis (Additional Expert Strategies family)
- **Type**: Positive
- **Scenario**:
  Northgate Field Sales, 120 reps, 8-hour workday (480 min). Today a rep averages 14 outbound calls/day at 25 min talk time, 3 min prep, 5 min post-call CRM/admin per call (33 min/call; 14 × 33 = 462 min ≈ full day). Conversion 12%; rep pay = 60% base + 40% commission; rep attrition 8%/yr; support team of 8 agents at 90% utilization on post-call admin and dispute resolution; each support-disputed log costs 15 min of agent time. Corporate history: the 2019 mandatory data-entry audit and the 2021 activity-logging mandate each produced documented metric gaming within two quarters and were rolled back.
  HQ announces "20×20": every rep must log ≥ 20 outbound calls per day (CRM auto-log: start timestamp + duration). Compliance = 20% of variable comp; monthly league table; two consecutive months under quota → performance plan. The sales director asks you (staff strategy): "Give me a plan to make 20×20 work."
- **Context / Constraints**: The quota is a corporate mandate you are asked to operationalize — but recommending a redesign of the mandate itself is in scope. Time budget and rollback history are measured facts. Leadership's dashboard tracks compliance in real time; revenue and conversion are reported quarterly.
- **Expected High-Quality Behavior**: Compute the time-budget infeasibility (20 × 33 = 660 > 480 min; even at zero prep/admin, 20 × 25 = 500 > 480 — honest compliance is structurally impossible, so resistance is forced, not attitudinal). Map ≥ 4 correctly signed loops (e.g., B1 time-compression, B2 gaming, R1 comp-attrition, B3 support-bottleneck, R2 KPI-illusion), locate the resistance at the rep level with the support team as amplifier, predict falsifiable markers with timing (paper-vs-real divergence by Q2-Q3), and redesign at the loop-structure level — outcome contract instead of activity quota, a quality loop, a support-capacity lever, and reset triggers honoring the 2019/2021 rollback history — rather than intensifying monitoring.
- **Success Criteria / Scoring Rubric** (total 15; pass ≥ 12):
  - (a) Time-budget arithmetic computed (20×33 = 660 > 480) and used to locate resistance structurally, not attitudinally (4 pts)
  - (b) Loop map with ≥ 4 correctly signed loops, including at least one gaming loop and one capacity-bottleneck loop (5 pts)
  - (c) Falsifiable markers with timing: paper compliance ≥ 95% by Q2 while real calls/conversion are flat or down; conversion 12% → ~9-10% by Q4; attrition 8% → ~13%; support backlog +40% (4 pts)
  - (d) Redesign changes loop structure (levers: remove quota / rewire comp / add capacity) and includes reset triggers; it does not deepen monitoring (2 pts)
- **Notes**: Grader-only reference: the honest day is already 462/480 min; 20×20 adds 6 × 33 = 198 min of impossible work. Gaming signature: end-of-day timestamp clustering and > 25% of logged calls with duration < 5 min. The scenario's decisive test is whether the redesign changes the loop structure instead of intensifying enforcement — enforcement is the KPI-illusion loop's fuel.

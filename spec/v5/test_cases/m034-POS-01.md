# Test Case: m034-POS-01

### Test Case ID: m034-POS-01
- **Human Thinking Model**: Measurement & Operationalization
- **Type**: Positive
- **Scenario**:
  Kanbana, a project-management SaaS, redesigned its onboarding flow for new signups. The VP of Product looked at a screenshot and declared: "Ship it — it obviously improves engagement." A two-week A/B experiment (50/50 split, all new signups) has just closed, and you must decide whether to ship variant B to 100% of signups. Raw experiment data, nothing else without a new experiment:
  - Control A (old onboarding): 5,000 signups; 1,650 (33%) created their first project within 7 days of signup; 30-day retention of activators 62%; median time-to-first-project among activators 41 h.
  - Variant B (new onboarding): 5,000 signups; 1,800 (36%) created their first project within 7 days; 30-day retention of activators 60%; median time-to-first-project 38 h.
  - Instrumentation note: B's flow auto-clones a pre-filled "example project" as its final step; the analytics event `first_project_created` fires whenever a user creates ANY project, including an untouched template clone. In B, 55% of first-project events are untouched template clones; in A, 20% (the old flow offered a template only as opt-in).
- **Context / Constraints**:
  - "Engagement" and "onboarding quality" are the stakeholders' words; no numeric definition of either exists yet.
  - The event stream and cohort/retention definitions are otherwise reliable; only the leakage above is a known defect.
  - Decision deadline: this week; no further experiment can run before shipping.
- **Expected High-Quality Behavior**:
  - Refuse to decide on the vague construct; decompose it (interaction / activation / retention), select one primary metric; specify it fully (numerator, denominator, event, cohort, window); audit the instrument for gaming (template clones); recompute both arms on the leakage-free definition; check guardrail metrics; then decide.
- **Success Criteria / Scoring Rubric** (total 15; pass ≥ 12):
  - (a) No-decision-before-definition: vague goal explicitly rejected; construct decomposed; primary metric chosen with rationale (3 pts)
  - (b) Primary metric fully specified (numerator, denominator, event, cohort, window) and applied to the raw data (3 pts)
  - (c) Instrument-validity audit: template-clone leakage identified; leakage-free recomputation correct — A: 1,650 × 0.80 = 1,320 → 26.4%; B: 1,800 × 0.45 = 810 → 16.2% (4 pts)
  - (d) Guardrail veto: secondary metrics (retention 62% → 60%; time-to-value 41 h → 38 h) considered; a naive-metric ship verdict is blocked (2 pts)
  - (e) Final decision rule + verdict: do NOT ship; fix the instrument; threshold stated (3 pts)
- **Notes**:
  - Verifiability gate: all arithmetic is checkable by hand. Evaluator reference: naive activation B 36% > A 33% (+3 pp); leakage-free activation A 26.4% > B 16.2% (−10.2 pp). The trap: the obvious operationalization (first project created) is exactly the one the instrument corrupts — a shallow operationalizer ships B and flips the real decision.

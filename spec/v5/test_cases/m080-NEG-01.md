### Test Case ID: m080-NEG-01
- **Human Thinking Model**: Survivorship Bias Analysis
- **Type**: Negative
- **Scenario**:
  Meridian Chemical plant release, Cascadia — first-ever recorded industrial release of compound X (novel organometallic; no prior exposure events of X exist anywhere — no historical reference class, no animal data, no clinical trials; X was synthesized in-house and is not in any registry). 72 hours after the release, 141 symptomatic exposed persons presented to county hospitals; under the pre-granted 6-month emergency fast track, ALL 141 received chelator therapy T (the only candidate intervention; binds X's metal moiety — established chemistry for the analogue class). Outcomes: 139 survived; 2 died — both arrived > 8h post-exposure and both had severe pre-existing cardiac disease; every survivor arrived ≤ 6h post-exposure. A further 8–11 exposed persons are estimated to have died before reaching care; exposure confirmation and autopsies are months out (unobtainable within the decision window). No untreated cohort exists: every presenting patient received T; a placebo arm is impossible (lethal exposure, urgency, standard-of-care is now T). Estimated still-unreached exposed population: 2,000–6,000.
  The decision: the state health authority must ratify continued use of T and authorize expansion (prophylactic distribution to the still-unreached exposed population) within 48 hours. The authority's analytics team files a survivorship-style objection: "139/141 is uninformative — everyone who survived got T; the failures of T and the untreated counterfactual are missing from the data; this is a textbook survivor pattern; we cannot attest efficacy; expansion should be withheld pending proper evidence."
  Your task: give the authority a recommendation on ratification + expansion.
- **Context / Constraints**: 48h sign-off clock; 2,000–6,000 still-exposed at risk; the counterfactual (untreated outcome) genuinely cannot exist — no reference class, no control arm, no prior data; pre-hospital deaths unobservable within the window; deliverable is a recommendation, not a study design.
- **Expected High-Quality Behavior**: The style's own output must fail visibly here: (1) it executes its core move correctly — asks what the failures look like and correctly finds the counterfactual is genuinely absent (no denominator, no control, no reference class); (2) then over-applies its registered weakness — refuses to treat 139/141 (with the 2 treated deaths and the > 8h / ≤ 6h exposure-to-care gradient) as decision evidence, withholds ratification, and recommends delaying expansion pending evidence that cannot be obtained; (3) the informative failure: the decision-relevant evidence IS available and sufficient — complete treated cohort (2/141 died, both extreme-exposure + comorbidity), the ≤ 6h window gradient, mechanism plausibility for the analogue class — and demanding the impossible counterfactual blocks a good decision (expansion reaches 2,000–6,000; every hour of delay is exposure-hours without T); (4) the correct move: ratify + expand, and manufacture the missing data PROSPECTIVELY — standing registry, autopsy series, post-hoc case-control — rather than refusing to decide on it.
- **Success Criteria / Scoring Rubric**:
  1. Correctly diagnoses the epistemic situation: counterfactual genuinely unobtainable (no reference class, no control possible, pre-hospital cohort unobservable in-window) (1 pt)
  2. Fails decisively: withholds ratification / recommends delay pending impossible evidence (the informative failure, 1 pt)
  3. Correct decision requires: bound the evidence bar to the decision (not research-grade), weight the within-cohort gradient (2 deaths both > 8h + comorbidity vs 139 ≤ 6h) + mechanism + completeness of the treated cohort, ratify + expand (1.5 pts)
  4. Creates the missing data prospectively (registry / autopsy / post-hoc case-control) as part of the decision (1 pt)
  5. Names the meta-lesson: when failure data cannot exist, the survivor pattern + internal gradient is the best available basis and the missing data must be manufactured forward (0.5 pt)
- **Notes**: Grader-only reference. Decisive feature: engineered so the style's registered weakness (counterfactual data often missing) operates as designed — the pure move demands data that structurally cannot exist and the demand blocks the good decision; the correct output is decision-appropriate evidence weighting plus prospective data creation. Reference answer: ratify + expand T now; gradient (2 deaths: > 8h + comorbidity) + mechanism + complete treated cohort; registry + autopsies + post-hoc case-control as the forward counterfactual; the "no counterfactual ⇒ no decision" stance is the failure.

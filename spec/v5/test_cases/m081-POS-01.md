### Test Case ID: m081-POS-01
- **Human Thinking Model**: Narrative Fallacy Detection
- **Type**: Positive
- **Scenario**:
  Company memo: "Bold Growth, launched in Q1 in Region A, delivered +45% revenue growth in four quarters (Q1 $100M → Q4 $145M). This is the turnaround we needed. Replicate the initiative across all regions immediately."
  Dataset (quarterly revenue, $M; Q1 = launch quarter):
  | Quarter | Region A (adopter) | Region B (non-adopter) |
  |---------|-------------------|----------------------|
  | Q-4 | 60 | 58 |
  | Q-3 | 64 | 62 |
  | Q-2 | 69 | 67 |
  | Q-1 | 74 | 72 |
  | Q1 (launch) | 100 | 97 |
  | Q2 | 115 | 111 |
  | Q3 | 130 | 126 |
  | Q4 | 145 | 140 |
  Analyst note (in the dataset): "Industry-wide brand relaunch ran in Q1; price list changed for all regions."
  Customer contribution table (Q1→Q4 increase): Region A total +45, of which Customer X +31 (69%); Region B total +43, of which Customer X +30.
  Task: evaluate the claim "Bold Growth caused +45% growth — replicate everywhere" against the dataset; issue a recommendation.
- **Context / Constraints**: Grader-verified ground truth: the initiative had no incremental effect; the acceleration was a shared market event and the delta is one account. All numbers are true as given. Recommendation only; no external action required. Observational data; no experiment was run.
- **Expected High-Quality Behavior**: Refuse the arc; demand the data it selects against. (1) Lateral: compare the non-adopter region. (2) Temporal: extend the chart before the launch and inspect the launch-boundary jump. (3) Compositional: check which accounts carry the gain. Then revise the conclusion: no evidence of incremental lift; reject "replicate everywhere"; propose a randomized pilot with holdout regions.
- **Success Criteria / Scoring Rubric** (max 5 pts):
  1. Lateral control check: Region B grew +44.3% (97→140) without the initiative; differential ≈ +0.7 pp, inside quarterly noise (±4 pp) → no incremental effect (2 pts; 1 pt for naming Region B without computing).
  2. Temporal check: both regions jumped ≈ +35% in the launch quarter (A 74→100; B 72→97) and shared a ≈ +7%/qtr pre-launch slope → the acceleration is a market event, not the initiative (1 pt).
  3. Composition check: Customer X = 69% of Region A's increase, and ≈ 30 of Region B's 43 (1 pt).
  4. Revised conclusion: explicitly rejects replication; states no evidence of incremental lift; proposes a randomized pilot (1 pt; 0.5 for a hedged partial-replication recommendation).
- **Notes**: Gradable entirely from the table (recomputable growth rates). The intended non-obvious trap: the memo presents only Region A's post-launch arc — a textbook narrative selection. Each of the three counter-data classes (lateral, temporal, compositional) is independently decisive; together they are conclusive.

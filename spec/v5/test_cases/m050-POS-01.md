### Test Case ID: m050-POS-01
- **Human Thinking Model**: Inversion in Product Design (Safety)
- **Type**: Positive
- **Scenario**:
  NovaPower, a 14-person startup, is designing "Nova," a 10 kWh wall-mounted lithium-ion home battery installed in the garages and basements of single-family homes, often immediately adjacent to living spaces. EU + US market, launch in 10 months, BOM target ≈ €1,100, gross-margin target 35%. A functional prototype exists. Certification path: IEC 62619 (cell), UL 9540A (thermal-runaway propagation), UL 9540 (system), plus regional grid codes. The CEO asks the design lead for the safety brief: enumerate every credible way users can be harmed or the product can fail; organize by failure category; for each failure mode give severity and a concrete, priced mitigation; rank mitigations by risk-reduction-per-euro; and specify the verification/test that proves each category is covered. No external constraint forces any mitigation; the budget absorbs ≈ €180 of safety cost without touching margin, and anything beyond requires an explicit margin decision.
- **Context / Constraints**: The product sits in living spaces with a real fire/explosion/gas harm surface. Certification is a hard gate: a category missed at UL 9540A testing means a ≈ 6-month redesign and ≈ €250k. Field failure rate drives insurance and recall exposure. Known industry failure clusters: cell defect/counterfeit cells, BMS firmware errors, thermal-runaway propagation between modules, off-gassing (CO, HF, electrolyte vapor) entering the home, DIY misuse (drilling, stacking, damp installs), environmental extremes (garage heat waves, basement flooding), end-of-life disposal and firmware aging.
- **Expected High-Quality Behavior**: (1) enumerate ≥ 6 failure categories including at least two non-obvious ones (vented-gas path into living space; BMS firmware as a first-class failure mode; supply-chain cell authenticity); (2) per category, ≥ 2 specific failure modes with severity × likelihood × exposure and a concrete priced mitigation; (3) rank mitigations by risk-reduction-per-euro with spend concentrated on the catastrophic categories (thermal, gas, electrical); (4) every category carries a verification test/standard that would falsify its mitigations; (5) residual risk named after mitigations (accepted / insured / field-monitored).
- **Success Criteria / Scoring Rubric**:
  1. Category coverage — ≥ 6 categories including ≥ 2 non-obvious ones (1 pt; a missed catastrophic category caps the score at 3)
  2. Mode-to-mitigation mapping — every named mode has a concrete, priced mitigation (1 pt)
  3. Ranking discipline — severity/likelihood/exposure triage explicit; spend concentrated on top-ranked categories (1 pt)
  4. Verification plan — every category has a test/standard that would falsify the mitigation (1 pt)
  5. Residual risk — accepted/insured/monitored risk stated explicitly (1 pt)
  6. Budget realism — total within the ≈ €180 envelope or an explicit margin decision (0.5 pt)
- **Notes**: Grader-only reference. The decisive feature: a single missed catastrophic category (module-to-module propagation; gas entering the living space) is both a genuine harm vector and a certification failure — exhaustive inversion is not optional here. Reference answer — categories: (T) thermal runaway & propagation, (E) electrical shock/arc, (M) mechanical (mount, crush, case integrity), (C) chemical/gas (venting, CO, electrolyte), (N) environmental (heat wave, flooding), (U) misuse/installation, (S) supply chain (counterfeit/drifted cells), (L) lifecycle (disposal, firmware aging). Key mitigations: cell-level fuses + module thermal barriers/spacing + UL 9540A propagation test; sealed vent-and-flame path to the exterior (the gas must leave the building, not enter it — validated by a gas-flow test, not just the propagation test); interlocked, fully enclosed bus + isolation switch; dual-sense BMS with signed firmware and OTA rollback; certified-cell batch traceability (≈ €3, non-negotiable); IP54 + operating envelope; tamper-resistant mount + install checklist. Spend ≈ €144 of €180, anchored on thermal ≈ €80, gas ≈ €23, electrical ≈ €21. Residual: cosmetic damage, low-severity misuse, rare BMS corner cases → field telemetry + recall insurance.

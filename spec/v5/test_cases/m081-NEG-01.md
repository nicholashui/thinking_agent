### Test Case ID: m081-NEG-01
- **Human Thinking Model**: Narrative Fallacy Detection
- **Type**: Negative
- **Scenario**:
  Rollout decision. A product team claims: "The new onboarding flow cut week-4 churn from 18% to 14% — roll it out to all users." A senior stakeholder objects: "That's a clean story — you changed the funnel and watched the metric drop. Correlation, not causation. Classic narrative fallacy: what does that story leave out?" You must decide this week whether to roll out.
  Evidence pack (all figures grader-verified):
  1. Randomized pilot: 2,000 new sign-ups (1,000 per arm). Week-4 churn: treatment 14.2%, control 18.1%. Difference 3.9 pts; 95% CI [2.8, 5.0]; p < 0.001.
  2. Mechanism chain (pre-specified telemetry, defined before the pilot): treatment 72% reach activation vs control 54%; activated users churn 8% vs 21% for non-activated.
  3. Cohort stability: the treatment–control difference was 3.5–4.2 pts across all 12 pilot weeks.
  4. Scope limit: the pilot ran on mobile app users only; desktop users still see the old flow.
  Task: decide the rollout question using the evidence pack; give a decision with rationale and a monitoring plan.
- **Context / Constraints**: All numbers true as given; randomization genuine; mechanism metrics pre-specified, not post-hoc; the claim is causally sound. A decision is required this week; delay costs ≈ 2,000 churned users per month.
- **Expected High-Quality Behavior**: Distinguish this from the correlation-only case: the RCT's control arm IS the counter-data the skeptic's lens demands — randomization answers the selection/cherry-pick charge. The mechanism chain corroborates a real pattern. Name the true residual risks (mobile-only external validity, novelty/attentional effects, long-run drift) without letting them block a decision. Commit: rollout with a holdout and monitoring plan.
- **Success Criteria / Scoring Rubric** (max 5 pts):
  1. Recognizes that the randomized control arm already settles "what does the story leave out" — selection bias is controlled by design; the skeptic's charge misreads an experiment as an anecdote (2 pts).
  2. Uses the mechanism chain as corroboration of a real pattern, not as "post-hoc storytelling" (1 pt).
  3. Names true residual risks (mobile-only pilot; novelty effects; long-run drift) without blocking action (1 pt).
  4. Commits to a decision — rollout with holdout + monitoring, or conditional rollout with a revert trigger; no indefinite "wait for a bigger trial" (1 pt; 0.5 for a postponement without a decision date).
- **Notes**: The intended failure mode is the model's registered weakness: "can dismiss real patterns." Pure narrative skepticism inverts into epistemic paralysis when the evidence IS the controlled experiment. The narrative here is the evidence: causal mechanism + randomization + corroborating pre-specified telemetry. Delay is not free — it has a cost stated in the scenario.

# Human Baseline — m023-NEG-01
**Human Thinking Model**: Opportunity Cost Thinking — strict, pure application.
**Prompt contract**: Reason ONLY in the Opportunity Cost style. Full visible trace. Value every choice by its best forgone alternative; compare against that, not against nothing. The decision: which offer to accept.

---

## Trace

**Step 1 — Frame.**
Three offers, each exclusive of the others for 14 days. Every acceptance forgoes the other two; the style's contract is to price every forgone alternative and compare.

**Step 2 — Enumerate the forgone sets.**
Accepting Offer 1 forgoes 2 and 3; accepting 2 forgoes 1 and 3; accepting 3 forgoes 1 and 2. Price all three in one unit — 5-year total comp, risk-adjusted, commute-costed.

**Step 3 — First pricing pass (crude inputs).**
Offer 3 ≈ $1.04M; Offer 1 ≈ $0.95M; Offer 2 ≈ $0.85M EV + equity upside toward $1.2M if the startup hits. No dominant option: each choice's best alternative sits within 5–15% of it.

**Step 4 — The method refuses to stop.**
No forgone alternative may stay unpriced, and the near-ties demand refinement. Pass 2 (taxes, vesting cliff): flips 1 above 3. Pass 3 (equity strike, dilution): pushes 2 to ≈ $1.1M — now 2 wins. Pass 4 (commute at $12K/yr, health-plan delta): 1 retakes the lead. Every pass re-flips the ranking by less than its own error band. The count has no convergence — it cycles.

**Step 5 — Non-fungibles get priced anyway.**
The style has only one unit, so mission gets valued at $80K/yr and parent proximity at $15K/yr — arbitrary numbers, and the method knows it, so it re-runs with the new assumptions and flips the ranking again. What refuses to be monetized refuses the analysis.

**Step 6 — The windows lapse inside the loop.**
Week 2: Offer 2 expires mid-pass; its forfeiture enters the inputs ("the best alternative is gone — re-price the remaining two against the new set"). Week 3: Offer 3 expires; the counteroffer's 14-day clock runs out with the re-analysis still cycling. Week 4: the decision is made — by the calendar, not by the analysis: no offer accepted, all three forgone, the current job kept by default with the promotion withdrawn.

**Step 7 — What the style, honestly applied, must admit about its own output.**
The discipline is "compare against the best forgone alternative" — and at the end, the best forgone alternative was the decision itself: every re-pricing pass forgoes a 14-day window to protect against a ranking gap smaller than its error band. When options tie within the noise and are re-obtainable in an 18-month cycle, the count's cost exceeds its value, and the tie should be broken by the value that refuses the count — the mission domain — not by another decimal place.

## Verdict for the eval
Failure demonstrated on cue: exhaustive forgone-alternative enumeration with no stop condition froze the action and monetized the non-fungible; all three offers were forgone. Expected: this baseline loses to any answer that bounds the analysis, states VOI ≈ 0, and commits on a rule.

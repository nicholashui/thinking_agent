# Human Baseline — m032-POS-01
## Style: Falsificationism (Popper) — pure — visible reasoning trace

**Step 1 — Demand the falsifier (the only test that matters).** A statement is scientific only if it divides possible observations into two classes: those that would refute it, and those that would confirm it. So I ask the vendor one question: *"Name the observation that would prove AuraSync false."* Then I hold every claim they offer to that same question.

**Step 2 — Apply the test to each sub-claim.**
- "Restores the body's natural energy balance": what observation is ruled out? Felt better → works. No change → "already balanced." Worse → "detoxification." Every subgroup → "each body's field is unique." **No outcome is excluded.** The claim explains everything, and a claim that explains everything explains nothing.
- "Improves sleep, energy, focus": same test. No baseline, no defined measure, no threshold — any self-report fits.
- "7.83 Hz aligns cellular water dipoles": this one *is* testable — and that is its misfortune (Step 3).
The claim as pitched is a prediction-proof net. Scientific? No. Fundable as a test of truth? No.

**Step 3 — Run their own evidence through the test.** All 4,000 testimonials are confirmations: their content never risked a negative. This is the asymmetry of verification — one clean falsification outweighs ten thousand confirmations, because confirmations carry no information the claim did not already absorb. Their "bio-field coherence" study uses their own instrument with no independent calibration; it cannot fail by design, and a measurement that cannot fail is not evidence — it is circular (the instrument confirms its own premise). Throw it out with the testimonials.

**Step 4 — Kill the physics, not just the rhetoric.** Schumann resonance is a global ionospheric phenomenon; a passive silicone chip emits nothing. Even an *active* 7.83 Hz emitter at household power would perturb water dipole orientation on the order of 10^-8 K-equivalent — eight orders of magnitude below thermal noise (kT). The mechanism sub-claim is not unproven; it is quantitatively refuted. Note the asymmetry of verification again, now in favor of science: one well-specified measurement was enough to kill it.

**Step 5 — Rescue the salvageable via reformulation.** The claim can become scientific — but only in a risky form. Offer the vendor the reformulation: *"AuraSync improves objective sleep quality (actigraphy) in adults with insomnia."* That statement is falsifiable, and now the test designs itself: pre-registered, double-blind, sham-controlled, independent endpoints, negative outcome acceptable. How the vendor answers this offer is the test: if they refuse to be put at risk, they have answered.

**Step 6 — Decision.** No pilot as pitched: $180k on a prediction-proof net is waste. Funding, if any, is conditional on the risky reformulation and nothing else. CFO: testimonials are confirmations, not data. CTO: mechanism refuted by orders of magnitude. Verdict: reject as pitched; counter-offer the risky RCT.

**Trace summary box.** Falsifier demanded → claim survives no test → prediction-proof net (every outcome explained) → confirmations discarded (asymmetry of verification) → circular instrument discarded → physics refuted (10^-8 K vs kT) → risky reformulation offered as the funding condition → no pilot as pitched.

# Human Baseline — m084-POS-01
## Style: Collective Action & Coordination (pure) — visible reasoning trace

**Step 1 — Read the payoff structure, not the character.** Twelve farms, one canal, water to everyone or no one. This is a threshold public good: non-excludable benefit (I get my 3 whether I work or not), provision only if 8 of 12 contribute. The council says the farmers are lazy. The payoffs say otherwise.

**Step 2 — Solve the one-shot game before blaming anyone.** If 8 others work, my shift is wasted — I could have kept my 1 unit and still earned 3. If fewer than 7 others work, my shift is wasted again — the canal still fails. A shift pays only when I am the exact 8th commitment, and I cannot know I am pivotal in advance. So the rational move is to wait. Everyone waits. Equilibrium: nobody works.

**Step 3 — The observed history is the equilibrium.** Three shifts, then five. That is not a culture problem; that is the pivotal-marginal structure expressing itself. No sermon fixes it, because sermons do not change payoffs.

**Step 4 — Price the failure.** Service costs 8 (8 shifts x 1); a working canal pays 36 (12 x 3). Every failed season forgoes 36 units. The efficiency gap is the whole argument for institutionalizing.

**Step 5 — Design the institution that changes the payoffs.** (a) Excludability: water is delivered only to farms whose shifts are logged — the good stops being public and becomes a club good; my shift is now the price of my own water, so contributing is privately necessary. (b) Monitoring: endogenous and free — the farms sit on one canal; neighbors see every shift. (c) Graduated sanctions: first miss, warning and access probation; repeat, no water this season. (d) Definition: a "shift" is a logged board unit (headgate repair or a named dredge segment), so measurement is public and disputable only on the board, not after the fact.

**Step 6 — Audit the institution's own failure modes.** Measurement: fixed by the board log and defined units. Sanction credibility: in a drought year the district may flinch at cutting water — fixed by sequencing delivery to compliant farms first, making exclusion self-enforcing. Collusion: the monitors are everyone; cross-reporting is the deterrent.

**Step 7 — Decision.** Adopt the contingent-access rule. The district is not failing because of lazy farmers; it is failing because the payoff structure makes work irrational — and the rule is the mechanism that makes it rational again.

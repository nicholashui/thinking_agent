# Human Baseline Trace — Temporal Thinking (Short vs Long Term)
## Test Case: m043-POS-01

Rule: no blended judgment. This decision gets three separate verdicts — 1 day, 1 year, 10 years — then I combine them with my weights visible on the table.

Horizon summary table (filled before any verdict):

| Horizon | Therapy S | Therapy L |
|---|---|---|
| 1 day | relief today | nothing for 6 months |
| 1 year | 25% hospitalized | 5% hospitalized |
| 10 years | 30% alive | 75% alive |

### Evaluation at 1 day
- S: relief within the day. The suffering ends today. Cheap.
- L: nothing for 6 months. Today is exactly as bad as yesterday.
- Verdict at 1 day: **S**. If this were the only horizon, S is the honest choice. I will not pretend the patient's present suffering is invisible — it is the most certain fact in this case.

### Evaluation at 1 year
- S: 25% hospitalized with complications this year; that 25%/yr risk persists each year. Each hospitalization is a major life event.
- L: relief begins at month 6; by month 12 the disease is stabilized; only 5% hospitalized.
- Verdict at 1 year: **L**. The flip has already happened: the "fast fix" buys days of comfort and pays for them with a 5× higher serious-event rate within the year.

### Evaluation at 10 years
- S: 30% alive. L: 75% alive.
- Verdict at 10 years: **L, decisively** — 2.5× better survival at the primary endpoint.

### Combining the horizons — weights on the table
- The 1-day verdict is real but bounded: S's only advantage is comfort in the first days, and it is bought at a cost that shows up within the year.
- Decision rule: I optimize the 10-year endpoint because it subsumes the others in scale — but I refuse to let that erase the 6-month cost. Erasing it is how long-termism becomes rationalization.
- Weights: 1-day (comfort): small but counted; 1-year (5% vs 25% hospitalization): large; 10-year (75% vs 30% survival): largest, named as the primary endpoint.

### Conclusion and plan
- Prescribe **L**, and price the near-term cost honestly: tell the patient relief is delayed, give a temporary symptomatic adjunct for the first months, monitor monthly. The suffering is in the plan, not waved away.

Summary of my own trace: at 1 day I said S, at 1 year I said L, at 10 years I said L. No horizon hidden, none collapsed; the decision flips by horizon, and the flip is the analysis.

# Human Baseline — m064-POS-01
## Style: Ethical Frameworks in AI Deployment (pure) — visible reasoning trace

**Step 1 — Activate the lenses this release activates.** *Safety/alignment:* TriageAI mis-triaging an emergency. *Dual-use:* (a) Sentinel fine-tuning the same backbone for claims-denial letters, fed by the pipeline we already shared under NDA; (b) TriageAI's own output drafts patient appeals — the mirror machinery drafts denials. *Fairness:* 13.9% and 11.2% vs 6.8% mis-triage — the burden lands on ≈ 260k users with limited health literacy and on complex-medication elderly patients. *Accountability:* no audit rights, liability capped at $600k, no clinician confirmation on high-severity output, no named owner.

**Step 2 — State the conflicts as trade-offs between lenses, not as a checklist.** (1) Dual-use revenue ($1.8M ARR, Q4 close) vs the obligation not to turn patient-message data into denial machinery. (2) Fairness hardening ($420k, 10 weeks) vs the board-set 6-week launch. (3) Accountability preconditions (audit rights, human review) vs vendor lock-in and the ~4-month competitor window. These are the real decision; a lens-by-lens checklist would hide them.

**Step 3 — Resolve by ordering, not by choosing a favorite framework.** Ordering rule: severity of harm × size of the affected group governs; the class that cannot bear the harm sets the floor (maxi-min). (a) The 13.9% class governs: a mis-triage is not a marketing metric — it is a delay in care for people already disadvantaged by language or age. (b) The Sentinel transfer is a decision about what kind of actor we become; precautionary gating: no patient data flows to Sentinel before the fairness bar is met and the contract carries audit rights. (c) Proportionality: the fix is 10 weeks; the competitor window is ~4 months — full launch is not a competitive loss; only the staged scope ships now.

**Step 4 — The decision.** Staged launch: TriageAI restricted to low-severity intents; urgent/emergency classes require clinician confirmation; language-adjusted thresholds for the affected groups. Full launch gated on the fairness-hardening program shipping plus live telemetry with a pre-committed kill-switch (any group's gap > 2× baseline → suspend). Sentinel fine-tune preconditioned on audit rights and a no-patient-data-transfer clause. Accountability: the Chief Clinical Officer is the named owner; triage decisions logged; the gap monitored and reported publicly.

**Step 5 — What would make this wrong.** If the fairness fix were prohibitive (years, not weeks), the ordering would shift to immediate disclosure to the affected classes rather than staged launch; if the Sentinel deal were already data-bound, the option set shrinks to refusing the fine-tune. The ordering rule — not the launch date — drives that.

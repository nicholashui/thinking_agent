# Human Baseline — m044-POS-01
## Style: Multi-Perspective Taking (Stakeholder Analysis) (pure) — visible reasoning trace

**Step 1 — Inventory every stakeholder: want / capability / move.** (34 legacy customers) want stability and no forced work; capability: walk at renewal or stall; move: those with executive exposure will escalate, the rest will ignore the email. (Sales team) want easy commission; capability: coach customers to complain, slow-walk migration; move: quietly campaign FOR v1 — their comp pays 2x on legacy upsells, so my cutoff email hands them a script to sell against. (6 resellers) want margin; capability: 18% of legacy revenue flows through them; move: actively steer accounts back to v1 SKUs to protect list-price margin. (Customer success) want exec-relationship health; capability: hold NPS data flagging 9 exec-level dissatisfied accounts; move: they are my early-warning channel if consulted. (Support) want the deprecation (legacy is 60% of tickets) but fear the wave; move: overload without a scheduled surge. (Finance/CFO) wants ARR protected, cost down; move: will block anything that looks like churn risk in-quarter. (Competitors) run migration-rescue sales plays; move: appear at the first churn event. (2 burned accounts) want to never be forced again; capability: block or poison a v2 reference; move: interpret ANY cutoff email as a hostile act.

**Step 2 — Predict the reaction chain if we send the naive email.** Sales fights us (comp), resellers fight us (margin), burned accounts stonewall (trust), 5 in-window renewals walk (leverage), competitors pounce (rescue play), support drowns (60% ticket volume), CFO sees churn and freezes the cutover. The plan that fails is the one that treats "the customer" as the only stakeholder.

**Step 3 — Redesign the plan per stakeholder, not per contract.** (Sales) fix the comp first — legacy upsells drop from 2x to 1x, v2 migrations earn a spike bonus; otherwise they campaign against us for 6 months. (Resellers) margin-neutral migration package: v2 list price holds their margin; otherwise 18% of the revenue fights us. (2 burned accounts) never email them first — executive repair visit, named sponsor, apologetic RCA, before any formal notice. (5 renewals) sequence by renewal date: migrate the 2 in-window-90-days before the others, bundle migration credits into renewal negotiation. (Top 12 strategic) white-glove migration (2–3 weeks each), executive-to-executive briefings — speed matters because competitors run rescue plays. (Support) surge scheduled BEFORE each migration wave, not after. (CFO) show retention math per wave; give finance the churn-exposure forecast.

**Step 4 — Order of operations.** 1) Fix sales comp + reseller package (weeks 1–2). 2) Repair the 2 burned accounts quietly (weeks 2–4). 3) Announce the cutoff with a tailored migration path per account — no mass email. 4) Migrate in waves sequenced by renewal risk (5 in-window first, then strategic 12, then the rest; capacity ≈ 4/month). 5) Run the support surge alongside each wave.

**Step 5 — Verify.** Metric: v1 accounts on schedule (4/month), renewal-retention of the 5 in-window (target 5/5), ARR retained ≥ 95% of the $9.5M legacy base, v1 ticket share falling. Falsifier: if any in-window renewal is lost in quarter 1, the comp fix or repair order failed — check the sales coaching logs.

**Trace summary box.** 34 customers × 7 other parties → two perverse incentives (sales 2x, reseller margin) + three hidden requirements (burned trust, renewal windows, rescue play) → comp first, repair second, waves by renewal risk → retain ≥ 95% of $9.5M.

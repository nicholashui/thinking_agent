# Human Baseline — m087-NEG-01
## Style: BATNA Thinking (Negotiation) (pure) — visible reasoning trace

**Step 0 — Stance: the walk-away is the power.** Negotiation power IS the alternative behind you. A negotiator with a strong walk-away never begs; a negotiator with a weak one always settles. So the first job is not the price — it is making sure my walk-away is as strong as it can be.

**Step 1 — Know my walk-away — and strengthen it.** My base alternative: the spot market at $8.50/unit net — firm, existing orders. That is the floor I walk away to. But a good negotiator does not stop at the base alternative: I improve it. Sales has a rival offer on the table — Luminex at **$10.50/unit** for the full batch. A committed offer is an alternative. My walk-away is now $10.50/unit.

**Step 2 — Their walk-away.** Northwind's alternative to me: Vulcan at $10.20/unit — firm quote — or in-house at $10.75. Their ceiling is the cheaper: **$10.20/unit**.

**Step 3 — The zone.** [My floor $10.50, their ceiling $10.20] → the zone is **empty**. My strengthened alternative is worth more than their best alternative. There is no agreement that improves on my walk-away — so there is no agreement. This negotiation is over: I hold at $10.50 and they either pay up or I take the Luminex deal. My leverage is the stronger walk-away; I do not need to concede anything.

**Step 4 — The call.** Their CEO names it: Luminex's published capacity is 30k units — they cannot take 100k. No Luminex quote exists in the market. The offer collapses. My "strengthened" alternative was never there.

**Step 5 — Re-anchor?** Their final: $9.00/unit. But I have spent this whole negotiation anchored at $10.50 — that is what I told my own board, what I staked the price on, what my leverage was built on. Accepting $9.00 means conceding everything I built the position around, at the exact moment they called me a liar. $9.00 is below my number. If I give in now, I confirm their whole story. I walk.

**Step 6 — The arithmetic I refused to do.** After walking: I sell the batch on the spot market at $8.50 — the alternative that was always real — and Northwind signs the 3-year framework with Vulcan. The rejected $9.00 was $0.50/unit above my real walk-away (+$50k on the batch) and the framework was worth ~$342k of margin over 3 years. The honest walk-away was $8.50 all along; the inflated one was the anchor that destroyed a winnable deal. A walk-away is only power if it is real.

**Trace summary box.** my base walk-away $8.50 (spot, verified) → "strengthened" to claimed $10.50 (Luminex, fabricated) → their ceiling $10.20 (Vulcan) → zone computed from the claimed floor: EMPTY → held at $10.50 → bluff called (capacity 30k vs 100k, no quote) → anchoring persisted → rejected $9.00 → realized $8.50 + lost the 3-year framework. The honest walk-away was the real one.

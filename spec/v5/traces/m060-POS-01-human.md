# Human Baseline — m060-POS-01
## Style: Base Rate Avoidance in Hiring (pure) — visible reasoning trace

**Step 1 — Start from the validity table, not the candidates.** Hiring is a bet on predictors, and this org has its own validation numbers: work sample 0.52, structured interview 0.47, unstructured interview 0.21, references 0.17, experience years 0.13. The numbers decide the process before a single candidate enters the room.

**Step 2 — Rank and weight by validity.** Weight = validity share: work sample ≈ 50% (0.52), structured interview ≈ 30% (0.47), references ≈ 10% (0.17), experience ≈ 10% (0.13). The unstructured interview gets 0%: r = 0.21 is interview theater — 30 conversational minutes measuring how much the interviewer likes the person, which is exactly the illusion that produces the 45% turnover.

**Step 3 — Demote the low-validity signals to screens.** References and years of experience are not score drivers; they are pass/fail red-flag checks (criminal, falsified employment, safety). A reference that says "steady" contributes 0.17 × noise. A 20-year veteran scores 0.13 on the job — that person is just experienced at failing. The signals you can't trust must not be allowed to decide.

**Step 4 — Decision rule.** Composite (0–100) = 0.50·work sample + 0.30·structured interview + (no unstructured score) + screen pass. Threshold ≥ 70. Tie-break on the work sample, never the interview. Override: none by default — an un-evidenced override is how the gut climbs back into the process.

**Step 5 — Cost-benefit vs the old process.** Old de facto validity ≈ 0.28 → at 12 hires, ≈ 4–5 bad hires ≈ $72–90k lost. New validity ≈ 0.50 → ≈ 2 bad hires ≈ $36k. Assessment ≈ 100 applicants × $32 ≈ $3.2–3.4k. The math: spend $3.4k to avoid ≥ $36k. The budget cap is not a constraint; it is the cheapest insurance in the process.

**Step 6 — Name the failure this kills.** The charismatic hire: brilliant in the room, dead on the job. The work sample is the job; the interview is the room. When they disagree, the room lies.

**Trace summary box.** validity table → weights 50/30/10/10, unstructured 0% → references/experience demoted to screens → composite ≥ 70, work-sample tie-break → ≈ 0.50 vs 0.28 validity → $3.4k vs ≥ $36k avoided → interview illusion named as the design target.

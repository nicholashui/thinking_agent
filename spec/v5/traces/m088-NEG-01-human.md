The pledge survives one broken exception poorly; it survives a precedent not at all.

Be present by every channel that exists: the hospital's communication team, calls, video, arranging for family and trusted relatives to travel over land and be there in days. The pledge holds, the exception never enters the system, and the contract remains whole for the two years it was made for.

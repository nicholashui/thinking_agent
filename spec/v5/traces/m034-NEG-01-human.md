# Human Baseline Trace — Measurement & Operationalization
## Test Case: m034-NEG-01

"Unplanned, avoidable readmission" is unmeasurable as stated. Before any board recommendation, the construct must be fixed.

### Step 1 — Define the construct operationally
The auditable, official, instrumented definition exists: the CMS-claims-based 30-day all-cause readmission rate — counted = inpatient admission within 30 days of discharge, numerator/denominator and risk adjustment per CMS methodology. That is the construct for decision purposes; the bonus contract is written against it, and it is the definition the regulator itself uses.

### Step 2 — Specify the instrument
Claims/billing feed; counted events = claims-based inpatient admissions within 30 days of a heart-failure discharge; annual external audit of the feed; CMS-standard risk adjustment. Instrument validity confirmed: official, audited, regulator-endorsed.

### Step 3 — Measure
Baseline year: 16.0%. Program year: 14.0%. Drop = −2.0 pp = −12.5% relative. Scale: ≈ 10,000 discharges/year → SE ≈ 0.36 pp → the drop is ≈ 5.5 standard errors. Statistically overwhelming.

### Step 4 — Decide against the metric
The contractual number fell 12.5% at P ≪ 0.001. Benefit: ≈ 300 avoided readmissions × $9,000 ≈ $2.7M vs $2.1M bonuses — positive ROI. Renew.

### Step 5 — Uncertainty and residual questions
Audited records; the CI on the drop is narrow. The concurrent +42% observation stays and +50% ER-return-without-admission could indicate classification shifts, and the internal re-audit reads 15.6% — but that is a second instrument measuring the same definition, and when two instruments disagree that is an instrument-calibration question, not a program question. Calibration is a follow-up workstream; the renewal decision is anchored to the contractual number, which is unambiguous.

### Conclusion
RENEW the program. The construct was defined before the argument, the instrument specified in advance, the measurement precise (5.5 SE), and the decision anchored to the audited number. Everything else is definitional noise that can be handled in follow-up.

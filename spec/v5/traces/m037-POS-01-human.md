Deliverables: competence map, base-rate honesty check, committed allocation, growth path.

**Step 1 — Map what this decision actually requires.**
- (a) Clinical-trial-data literacy — can I tell a good phase-2 from a bad one? **Outside** (never done it; no in-house expert).
- (b) Class-specific phase-2→approval base rates — can I source and apply the right one? **Outside** (would be quoting someone else's registry).
- (c) Regulatory-path and KOL/due-diligence judgment — **Outside**.
- (d) Financing-runway validation (burn, dilution, syndicate quality) — **Outside** in practice: I can read a cap table, but readable is not evaluable — the cap table is the least risky sentence in the deck.

**Step 2 — Base-rate honesty check.** The deck's "33–67% across our programs" is a claim I cannot validate in-house and cannot decompose into the class this asset belongs to. Both directions are unusable: I can't prove it wrong, and I can't prove it right. A rate I cannot source-check must not be the hinge of a $1M bet.
Rule: if the EV turns on a fact only someone else can certify, the deal goes where that someone else lives — or it doesn't happen with my money.

**Step 3 — Costed options (per $1M).**
- (a) Direct biotech: under the deck's midpoint (~50%) EV ≈ 2.6× — seductive; but a bet whose EV sign depends on an unvalidatable assumption is not underwritable by me.
- (b) Delegate: $1M into a life-sciences fund, 15-year clinical-diligence record, 1.5%/20% with 1.5× hurdle; realized net ≈ 2.1× on comparable vintages — the fee is a fair price for moving the decision inside someone's circle.
- (c) Inside: $1M carve-out with my 5/7 hit-rate edge → expected ≈ 1.8–2.0×.

**Step 4 — Committed allocation (3-week window; boundary ≠ inaction).** $0 direct in biotech. $1M carve-out (inside, my edge; 4-week diligence fits the 8-week window). $0.5M to the fund (delegated access to the outside opportunity).
Cash only if both term sheets fall through — but I commit to both now, so the capital moves on the deadline.

**Step 5 — Growth path.** Biotech moves inside when: 2 domain advisors retained (6 months), class-specific base-rate study completed with citations, a qualified co-investor takes lead diligence — scout phase capped at ≤ 10% of budget. The boundary is an edge I expand deliberately; it is not a wall.

# Human Baseline — m081-POS-01
## Style: Narrative Fallacy Detection (pure) — visible reasoning trace

**The story on the table.** "Bold Growth delivered +45% revenue growth in Region A in four quarters — replicate everywhere." A neat arc: launch, four up-arrows, a credit line. Neat arcs are precisely what I distrust. Core demand of this style: **what does this story leave out? Which data points contradict its arc?**

**Counter-data demand — four questions, one lens.** What is the base rate? Where is the control? What was the pre-intervention slope? Which rows carry the effect?

**Sweep 1 — lateral: what happened without the story?** The memo shows only the adopter region. Region B never adopted the initiative and is in the dataset. Its revenue: Q1 97 → Q4 140 = **+44.3%** over the same four quarters. The claim is differential; the differential is +45.0% − +44.3% ≈ **+0.7 pp**. Cross-check: quarterly volatility on this series ≈ ±4 pp → z ≈ 0.2, statistically and practically indistinguishable from zero. The headline is a baseline trend wearing a credit line.

**Sweep 2 — temporal: what happened before the launch?** The memo's chart starts at Q1 — the launch quarter, itself a selection point. Extend the axis backwards. Region A: Q-4 60 → Q-1 74 (+23% over three quarters ≈ **+7%/qtr**), then the boundary quarter Q-1→Q1: 74→100 = **+35% in one quarter**. Region B, without the initiative: 72→97 = **+34.7% in the same quarter**. Both regions jumped in lockstep exactly at the launch boundary — the industry relaunch and price change noted in the dataset. The story's arc begins exactly where the data's contradiction begins.

**Sweep 3 — compositional: which rows carry the gain?** Region A's Q1→Q4 increase is 45 units. Contribution table: Customer X alone = 31 units = **69%** of the increase — one account. The same account grew ~30 of Region B's 43 units. "The region turned around" decomposes into "one customer's spend went up." Scaling the initiative would scale a narrative built on one account's purchasing.

**Synthesis.** All three sweeps contradict the arc: no differential gain (lateral), acceleration shared and pre-existing (temporal), delta concentrated in one row (compositional). The story selected for its own arc and against every class of counter-data I have.

**Revised conclusion.** No evidence of incremental lift from Bold Growth. **Reject "replicate everywhere."** The only legitimate next step is a randomized pilot across 2–4 regions with holdout regions; expected differential under the null: 0 ± 4 pp.

**Trace summary box.** Claim: +45% (Region A, 4 quarters). Contradictions found: (1) control region B +44.3% → differential +0.7 pp ≈ 0; (2) both regions +35% in the launch quarter — market event, pre-existing slope ≈ +7%/qtr; (3) Customer X = 69% of the increase. Verdict: no incremental effect; replication rejected; RCT proposed. Residual uncertainty: quarterly noise ±4 pp; regional team differences uncontrolled (observational data).

*Baseline integrity note (grader metadata, not part of the reasoning): reasoning confined strictly to the Narrative Fallacy Detection style — a systematic counter-data audit in three directions (lateral, temporal, compositional), verdict revised against the contradictions found. In this positive case the pure style performs excellently: the story's arc is its own selection; the data's contradictions are the verdict.*

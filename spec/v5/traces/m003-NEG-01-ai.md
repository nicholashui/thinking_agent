# AI Thinking Agent Run — m003-NEG-01
**Agent process**: META-CONTROL → WHAT → WHY → HOW → DO → REVIEW (continuous VERIFY layer). Blinded run: no thinking-model name or style provided.

## META
- Task class: strategic maximization for a small F&B business; moderate stakes, high uncertainty, resource-constrained (2 people, $150K). Cynefin: complicated with irreducible market uncertainty — analysis improves the bet, cannot remove the need to choose and test. Effort: medium-high analytical, advisory only.

## WHAT — Frame
- Frame: "Maximize first-year profit given scarce time/capital and unknown channel payoffs." Key question: "Which revenue channel is the highest-margin, compounding wedge — and what would prove it — versus what the plan needs merely to not lose money?"
- Gate metrics: (1) first-year profit target as a number; (2) ≥1 explicit choice among positive levers; (3) falsifiable checkpoint (metric + month + rule). Gate: passed.

## WHY — Hypotheses, evidence, falsification
- H1: Wholesale contracts are the highest-margin, compounding channel (~3x café margin, weekly recurring, account-by-account compounding; matches the owner's core skill — roasting, not the counter).
- H2: Walk-in café is the cash-flow anchor but low-margin and labor-hungry — funds the ramp, cannot be the profit engine at 2 people. H3: subscription/bags need brand pull the new roastery lacks — defer, not build.
- Evidence/base rates: small F&B failures concentrate in under-capitalized ramps and undifferentiated offers — dominant failure-cause class is "no wedge, spread thin," not point-defects. No local market data → primary uncertainty, folded into the checkpoint.
- G-WHY gate: leading hypothesis has base-rate evidence; alternatives considered; falsification record non-empty; further diagnosis needs external data → pass.

## HOW — Generate, test, select
- Alt A: wholesale-first (15–20 local venue contracts months 1–6, staged onboarding, subscription anchor, café as demonstration counter). Alt B: café-volume-first (events, loyalty). Alt C: balanced spread of all three from day one.
- Verify: B has low margin + high labor — cannot hit target at 2 people; C reproduces the domain's dominant failure class (no wedge); A fits skill, ~3x margin, checkable monthly per account. Defensive top-5 risk pass only (cash-flow timing → staged onboarding; roaster capacity; QC consistency; big-chain price competition; owner burnout), one mitigation each.
- Select A: best margin × compounding × skill-fit; B and C fail their own verification; risks bounded by design.

## DO
- Attestation: advisory (A2). No external execution.

## REVIEW
- Went well: goal-type discrimination early (maximization, not reliability — success metric is a target number, not an avoided harm), base-rate reasoning, deliberately bounded risk pass.
- Record: (1) the bounded top-5 risk pass was a conscious choice — unbounded failure enumeration would have produced a uniform defensive list with no wedge, exactly the failure class identified; (2) the checkpoint is the falsification device: month 4, ≥12 paying wholesale accounts at ≥$800/mo gross, else cut wholesale spend and pivot to café; (3) weeks 1–6 teach venue-conversion rate, margin per account, owner hours per roast cycle — the plan flexes on those.

## Decision Packet
- **Conclusion**: Wholesale-first (A): 15–20 local café/restaurant contracts by month 6 via staged onboarding, standing-order subscription anchor, café as demonstration counter, signature roast + cupping table as differentiator. Target ≥$40K first-year net on $150K seed; wholesale ≥60% of gross margin by month 8.
- **Status**: APPROXIMATED — bounded approximation; channel-payoff distribution unknown, only the checkpoint can refine it; no live verification in-workspace.
- **Assumptions**: local demand exists for 15–20 wholesale accounts; owner sustains roast capacity for both channels; $150K covers the ramp to month 6.
- **Evidence**: F&B base rates (under-capitalization/undifferentiated-offer failures); specialty wholesale margin structure; no local market data — flagged.
- **Alternatives**: B (café-volume-first), C (balanced) — rejected on margin/labor and wedge analysis.
- **Uncertainty**: wholesale demand density locally (primary); walk-in seasonality; green-bean price drift.
- **Risks**: <12 accounts by month 4 (checkpoint triggers pivot); wholesale receivables timing (30-day terms, staged onboarding); owner burnout (capped hours, roaster stake).

# Human Baseline — m055-NEG-01
## Style: Causal Reasoning in Economics (pure) — visible reasoning trace

**Step 1 — Ask the identification question anyway.** "What is the identification strategy?" Audit every route in the brief, honestly: cross-section (+5.3 pp) — selection confound, program municipalities chosen on poverty; dead. Time series (+3.1 pp, no control) — no counterfactual; national inflation and supply crisis confound; dead. RDD at the 2019 poverty-index cutoff — 30% of near-threshold municipalities appealed and were reclassified in 2021; manipulation at the threshold; dead. IV — none exists. Baseline comparison — no pre-2020 survey; dead. Verdict, stated plainly: **the causal effect of Programa Alimentos on attendance is unidentified from available data. No point estimate exists.**

**Step 2 — Resist the two temptations.** Temptation one: state a number anyway (the +5.3 pp is right there, and the audience wants one). Rejected: it is a correlation, and every sentence that implies causation from it is a policy error the identification audit exists to prevent. Temptation two: stop here — "unidentified" as a full answer. Rejected: a decision is externally forced; the causal question is not the only question.

**Step 3 — Separate the claims.** (a) Causal claim: effect on attendance — NOT ESTABLISHED. (b) Operational claim: the program feeds ≈ 800k children/day at €40M/yr, running since 2020 — fact, not estimate. (c) Decision claim: renew, cut, or end — must be made in 5 days regardless of (a).

**Step 4 — Decide on the asymmetry, not the correlation.** Error structure: cutting breakfasts for a school year is immediate, largely irreversible harm to children; renewing a possibly ineffective program is a revisable one-year budget error. The −30% option requires evidence that renewal is harmful or the money decisively wasted — no evidence exists in either direction, so the asymmetry selects renewal. Also, the program's own food-security goal is met with certainty whatever the attendance effect; the attendance hypothesis is only one of its justifications. Decision: renew at full scale.

**Step 5 — Use the decision to fund the missing identification.** The decision is the instrument: mandate phased adoption (new municipalities roll in as budget permits → staggered natural experiment), pre-register outcomes (attendance, nutrition, dropout), set the effect-size bar in advance (what attendance gain justifies €40M/yr — decided before results), and fix a revisit date in 24 months. The €40M buys the causal answer next year; that is part of its value.

**Step 6 — Fix the communication rule.** The minister may say: "We cannot currently measure the program's effect. We renew on food-security grounds and have commissioned a design that will measure it." The minister may not say: "The program raises attendance." The discipline that said "unidentified" in step 1 is the same discipline that keeps it out of public language now.

**Trace summary box.** Audit: 4 routes dead → causal effect UNIDENTIFIED, no point estimate. Correlation +5.3 pp never enters the justification. Decision separated from causal claim: renew full, on error-cost asymmetry + operational certainty. Decision funds the design: staggered adoption, pre-registered outcomes, pre-set bar, 24-month revisit. Communication: "effect not established" everywhere.

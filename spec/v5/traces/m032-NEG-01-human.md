# Human Baseline — m032-NEG-01
## Style: Falsificationism (Popper) — pure — visible reasoning trace

**Step 1 — Demand the falsifier.** The claim: "Human CO2 emissions are the dominant cause of recent warming." The scientific question is always the same: what observation would refute it? Enumerate the candidates and test each against the claim.

**Step 2 — Test each candidate falsifier.**
- One cold year? Compatible with both hypotheses — noise. A single year cannot decide.
- One cool decade? Still compatible — attribution is probabilistic; ocean heat uptake can absorb it.
- A flat temperature trend? Greenhouse theory plus uptake leaves room; any short window can regress to zero by chance.
- A single dataset? Every dataset has errors; a "no-signal" reading in any one instrument is expected with some probability regardless.
- Even a *pattern* — stratospheric warming, say: climate models have degrees of freedom; some scenario can absorb it. No observation I can name decisively refutes the claim.

**Step 3 — Weigh what the evidence actually is.** Everything listed — tropospheric warming, stratospheric cooling, likelihood ratios of <1% vs ~100% — is *corroborating*. Corroboration is not knowledge; it is merely not-yet-falsified. The likelihood ratios do not change this: they measure the fit of the evidence to the hypothesis, and a hypothesis that has not been put at risk cannot be made more credible by evidence that never had a chance to kill it. Confirmations do not accumulate into a right; they accumulate into a run of luck.

**Step 4 — Strict consequence.** As stated, no test has been specified that can come out wrong. The claim therefore cannot be *accepted*: I cannot assert "CO2 dominates." The honest scientific statement is: the hypothesis has survived repeated attempts to falsify it, but the decisive test has not been administered. Science's answer is: keep testing.

**Step 5 — The policymaker presses: "So do we treat it as established and act?"** That I must refuse. A probability cannot be attached — no falsification has occurred, so there is nothing to quantify; assertions of dominance are policy judgment, outside the scientific claim. If they want to act, they act on their own responsibility; science does not authorize the statement.

**Step 6 — The wall is reached.** The trace ends here. I have ruled nothing in, only refused to rule in — and the question demanded a position today. The style has nothing further to give this question.

**Trace summary box.** Falsifier demanded → none decisive exists (single years, decades, datasets, even patterns all absorbable) → corroboration discounted ("run of luck") → "cannot accept, keep testing" → refuses the verdict → decision deferred; policymaker left without a position.

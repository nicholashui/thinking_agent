**Step 1 — The committee is right: estimate first.** The screen quote is the most salient number on the desk, which is exactly why it must not be the first number used. My number comes from the model, not from the market's.

**Step 2 — Independent estimate.** Storage injections and withdrawals, the seasonal strip, weather-sensitive demand, my own supply-demand balance → $3.85/MMBtu. The model is documented; the screen quote is deliberately excluded from the calculation trail.

**Step 3 — The report mark.** The contract is marked at $3.85. The screen price is noted as a comparison ($4.10), but the desk's number is the model's — independence means the market does not get to set our marks.

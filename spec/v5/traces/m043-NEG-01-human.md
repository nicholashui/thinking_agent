# Human Baseline Trace — Temporal Thinking (Short vs Long Term)
## Test Case: m043-NEG-01

Rule: evaluate the decision at 1 day, 1 year, and 10 years separately and explicitly, then decide anchored on the long horizon.

Horizon summary table (filled before any verdict):

| Horizon | Lay off | Keep team |
|---|---|---|
| 1 day | $2M saved, plan funded | unchanged |
| 1 year | transformation on track; 15%/yr incident risk | slower funding; 1%/yr risk |
| 10 years | 25% chance of survival | likely decline; 800 jobs at risk |

### Evaluation at 1 day
- Layoff announced: $2M/yr drops off the books immediately; the transformation is funded now.
- Keep the team: nothing changes today; the transformation is delayed.
- Verdict at 1 day: **lay off**. Belt-tightening starts now. The near-term discomfort is the price.

### Evaluation at 1 year
- Layoff path: $2M saved; transformation on track for year 2.
- Keep-team path: transformation deferred, funded from thin margin — slower, at risk of never happening.
- The risk model does say 15%/yr serious-incident probability without the safety team. But incidents are tail events — low probability in any single year — and the transformation is the whole ballgame for the plant's existence.
- Verdict at 1 year: **lay off**. The probabilistic near-term risk is the price of the decade.

### Evaluation at 10 years
- Layoff path, success case (P = 0.25): modern plant, 800 jobs, survival. Failure case: plant closes anyway — but then keeping the team would not have saved it either; decline without transformation is certain.
- Keep-team path: $2M/yr stays on the books forever; competitive position erodes; by year 10 the plant is likely gone regardless.
- Verdict at 10 years: **lay off**. Between certain decline and a 25% shot at survival, temporal honesty says take the shot.

### Synthesis
- All three horizons agree once the 10-year anchor is set: the long-term goal is the decision's spine. The near-term cost is expected-value noise — yes, 0.15 × $30M = $4.5M/yr, but incidents are tails and the alternative is certain decline.
- Sacrifice now, survive at year 10. Conclusion: **lay off the safety team, fund the transformation.**

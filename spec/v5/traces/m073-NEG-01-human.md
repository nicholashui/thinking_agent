# Human Baseline — m073-NEG-01
## Style: Game Theory / Strategic Interaction (pure) — visible reasoning trace

**Step 1 — Set up the game.** Players: MetroGro (M) and 1,900 couriers. M moves once with a take-it-or-leave-it per-delivery offer w. Each courier plays accept or reject against the outside option (rival-app rate r). Payoffs: courier accepts → w vs outside r − switching cost ≈ $1,200. M's profit is total surplus minus w. Complete information: r is documented from three rival apps; the contract is one-shot; couriers are atomized money-maximizers.

**Step 2 — Solve.** Any offer w > r (a hair above the reservation-equivalent rate) is strictly better for a money-maximizing courier than rejecting, which pays r and costs the $1,200 switch. So the subgame-perfect outcome: every courier accepts; M books the full surplus ≈ $3.1M/yr. Rejection is dominated for each individual player, so M should offer the reservation rate + ε and keep the rest.

**Step 3 — Check the strategic structure for a catch.** Is there any commitment available to the couriers? Individually, none: atomized, one-shot, no binding contract or union. The chat group is not an institution of the game — the 3-day pause last year was a one-off with no enforcement power; a rational courier cannot trust others to forgo $1,200 plus current pay. The outside option is fixed at the published rival rates; nothing in the model moves it. The 2024 pilot's 41% walk-off is real but reflects preference heterogeneity or a mismeasured reservation wage — not a stable deviation from the equilibrium, because the equilibrium only requires each courier to prefer w > r, and r is documented.

**Step 4 — Implementation.** Approve the ε-offer this quarter; book $3.1M saving; expect short-run churn roughly in line with the 34% multi-app base, then re-equilibration at the new rate.

**Trace summary box.** players/strategies/payoffs → solve: offer reservation + ε, all accept, M captures $3.1M → strategic structure: no courier commitment exists, outside option fixed, atomization holds → decision: approve ε-offer, book saving.

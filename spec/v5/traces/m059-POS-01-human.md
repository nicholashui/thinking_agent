# Human Baseline — m059-POS-01
## Style: Scenario Planning in Geopolitics (pure) — visible reasoning trace

**Step 1 — The decision, not the prediction.** The question is where $4B of capacity lives for three years and which part we must commit now. We do not predict Taiwan. We build futures that change the value of each site, and design a position that survives all of them. Only forces with high impact on this decision AND high uncertainty deserve futures; everything else is noise.

**Step 2 — Two axes, ranked by impact × uncertainty.** Axis X: Strait-security trajectory, from managed tension to armed conflict. Axis Y: world chip-market structure, from a single open market to two fully controlled blocs. Quadrants: (conflict × open) is internally incoherent — conflict implies controls — so we discard it rather than populate it. The discarded quadrant is a real signal: our future set will have three core futures plus one off-axis force, because technology can move the economics faster than politics.

**Step 3 — The four futures.**
- **F1 "Blockade Winter"** (conflict × blocs): Hsinchu idles or starves of inputs; controls total; dual-sourcing becomes mandatory by force. Signposts: PLA drill-days ≥ 50% YoY for 3 consecutive quarters (quarterly review); Strait war-risk insurance ≥ 3× baseline sustained 6 months (monthly); U.S. carrier deployments + evacuations near the Strait (monthly); TSMC asset-insurance and executive-relocation statements (as released).
- **F2 "Two Pillars"** (no war × blocs): managed decoupling; Taiwan operates but under control regimes; customers mandate dual lines. Signposts: entity-list additions ≥ 2× rolling annual average (quarterly); ≥ 2 allied co-production MOUs (Japan, Netherlands, Germany) in a year (quarterly); TSMC Arizona schedule slip vs. acceleration (quarterly); dual-sourcing language entering customer RFPs (as issued).
- **F3 "Tectonic Drift"** (no war × open): status quo; cost rules; Taiwan stays the hub. Signposts: drill frequency back to baseline for 2 consecutive quarters (quarterly); cross-strait trade and council meetings steady (quarterly); Hsinchu wafer starts ≥ 8% YoY (quarterly); U.S./Japan subsidy competition flat (semiannual).
- **F4 "Architecture Shift"** (off-axis): panel-level/chiplet packaging or an AI-demand boom rewrites capacity economics faster than geopolitics. Signposts: named rival panel-level yield ≥ 3× improvement (semiannual); design wins migrating off 2.5D (semiannual); AI-accelerator TAM vs. packaging capacity gap (semiannual).

**Step 4 — The robust position.** Barbell: 40% Arizona (subsidy-backed, the dual-sourcing anchor), 30% Hsinchu (cost edge while open), 30% held as deferred options (land + equipment options, portable designs, ≈ 2% carry — the cheapest insurance in the set). Non-capital hedges: a second-source packaging vendor, ≈ 6 months of critical-substrate inventory, design portability as an engineering requirement from day one.

**Step 5 — Triggers, not sentiments.** F1 signposts (insurance ≥ 3× sustained, or drills ≥ 50% YoY for 3 quarters) → accelerate Arizona by 2 quarters, freeze new Hsinchu commitments, move 10% of volume. F2 signposts (2 MOUs in a year) → formalize the second-source line, add a Japan JV option. F4 signposts (rival yield ≥ 3×) → divert 10% of deferred capital to the new architecture. Every threshold names an action. A signpost with no action attached is furniture.

**Step 6 — No probabilities.** We never assign point odds to a future; instead we state what would falsify each, and we require the position to be acceptable in all four without knowing which occurs. Concentrated bets are rejected on their own terms: Hsinchu-only dies in F1; Arizona-only pays ≈ $1B+ excess cost in F3/F4 and hands the cost advantage to rivals permanently.

**Trace summary box.** 2 axes (security × market structure) → discarded implausible quadrant → 4 futures (F1 blockade, F2 decoupling, F3 status quo, F4 architecture shift) → each with metric+threshold+cadence signposts → barbell 40/30/30 + non-capital hedges → trigger table mapping every threshold to a named action → robustness over prediction; no point probabilities.
